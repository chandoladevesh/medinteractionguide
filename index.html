How MedInteractionGuide Works

Drug Information

Normalize medicine names with the U.S. National Library of Medicine RxNorm service before searching labels.

Interaction Evidence

Review relevant text from FDA drug-label interaction sections and distinguish a label mention from an absence of evidence.

Source Transparency

See the FDA openFDA query used to retrieve the underlying label information.

Important: MedInteractionGuide is an informational tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. A label mention is not a personalized risk assessment, and a missing mention does not establish that an interaction does not exist.
